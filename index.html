About KRXGEN'26

KRXGEN'26 is a National Level 24-Hour Hackathon organized by the Department of Mechanical Engineering, K. Ramakrishnan College of Engineering.

The event aims to inspire students to transform innovative ideas into practical engineering solutions through collaboration, creativity, design thinking, and technology.

Participants will work in teams to solve real-world industrial challenges under expert mentorship and present their innovative prototypes before an experienced jury panel.
