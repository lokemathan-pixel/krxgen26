Official Rules & Regulations

Theme 1 – Sustainable Energy Technology

Theme Objective

Develop innovative, practical and sustainable engineering solutions that improve energy generation, storage, distribution, efficiency and environmental sustainability.

Eligible Problem Areas

  • Renewable Energy Systems (Solar, Wind, Biomass, Hydro, Geothermal)
  • Smart Energy Management
  • Energy Storage Systems (Battery, Supercapacitor, Hydrogen)
  • Electric Vehicle Charging Solutions
  • Waste-to-Energy Technologies
  • Green Manufacturing
  • Carbon Footprint Reduction
  • Smart Grid Technologies
  • Energy-efficient Buildings
  • AI / IoT for Energy Optimization
  • Sustainable Agriculture Energy Solutions
  • Rural Electrification

Team Formation

  • Each team shall consist of 3–4 members.
  • Students from AICTE-approved institutions are eligible.
  • Interdisciplinary teams are encouraged.

Prototype Requirement

  • Working prototype, simulation, software model or proof-of-concept.
  • No copied or commercial projects are allowed.

Innovation

  • Projects must be original.
  • Solutions should address real-world sustainability challenges.
  • Open-source libraries may be used with proper acknowledgement.

Technical Scope

  • Mechanical Systems
  • Electrical Systems
  • Electronics
  • Internet of Things (IoT)
  • Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
  • Robotics
  • Embedded Systems
  • Mobile / Web Applications
  • CAD / CAE Simulation
  • Digital Twin Technology

Sustainability Criteria

  • Energy Saving
  • Renewable Energy Utilization
  • Pollution Reduction
  • Carbon Emission Reduction
  • Resource Conservation
  • Circular Economy
  • Sustainable Manufacturing

Deliverables

  • Abstract (Maximum 300 words)
  • Problem Statement
  • Proposed Solution
  • PPT Presentation
  • Working Prototype / Simulation
  • Source Code
  • Bill of Materials (BOM)

Safety

  • Unsafe electrical systems are prohibited.
  • High-voltage equipment must be properly insulated.
  • No hazardous chemicals or explosive materials.
  • Teams are responsible for prototype safety.

Presentation

  • 5 Minutes Presentation
  • 5 Minutes Question & Answer Session

General Guidelines

  • Bring your own laptop and required hardware.
  • Internet access will be provided if available.
  • Carry a valid College ID card.
  • Plagiarism will result in immediate disqualification.
  • Judges' decisions are final.
  • Organizers may modify the schedule if necessary.

Expected Outcomes

  • Engineering Innovation
  • Practical Problem Solving
  • Sustainable Development
  • Technical Excellence
  • Team Collaboration
  • Social & Environmental Impact
THEME 1

Theme 2 – Augmented Reality & Virtual Reality

Theme

Develop innovative AR, VR or Mixed Reality (XR) solutions that solve real-world problems. Projects may belong to education, healthcare, gaming, manufacturing, smart cities, cultural heritage, training or any creative XR application.

Eligibility

  • Open to students from recognized colleges and universities.
  • Each team shall consist of 3–4 members.
  • Cross-disciplinary teams are encouraged.

Technical Guidelines

  • Projects must be completed before the hackathon.
  • On-the-spot development is not allowed.
  • Projects should be developed using Unity Game Engine.
  • Use ARCore, ARKit or OpenXR wherever applicable.
  • Explain problem statement, solution, technology and future scope.

Demonstration

  • VR projects will be demonstrated using Meta Quest headsets provided by organizers.
  • Participants should bring laptops capable of running their VR application.
  • AR projects should be demonstrated using participants' own smartphones.

Presentation

  • 10 minutes demonstration
  • Technical explanation
  • Question & Answer session

Evaluation Criteria

Criteria Weightage
Practical Impact 30%
Use of AR / VR Technology 25%
Problem Understanding 20%
Innovation 15%
Presentation 10%

General Rules

  • Online registration is mandatory.
  • PPT must be submitted before review.
  • Projects must be original.
  • Plagiarism leads to disqualification.
  • Professional conduct is expected.

Disqualification

  • Copied work
  • False claims
  • Unsafe demonstrations
  • Violation of event rules
